Video Time Capsule (VTC)
Founder & Lead Designer · 2023 – 2025
View details ↓
A senior-friendly digital archive platform allowing elders to preserve and share family stories with younger generations.
Overview & Design Impact

Video Time Capsule (vtcmemories.net) focuses on making story recording natural and intuitive for senior citizens. The interface incorporates high-contrast visual cues, large touch targets, and streamlined UX flows to eliminate technical friction.

This project spearheaded my passion for human-centered UI/UX design and demonstrated how empathetic accessibility choices can bring emerging technology to underrepresented user demographics.

Tech & Skills: UI/UX Prototyping, Accessibility Design, Product Design, Web Development.

Humans or Haptics – Sports Training Research
Published in NHSJS · 2022 – 2023
View details ↓
Peer-reviewed independent research investigating biomechanical and cognitive metrics in robotic vs. human sports coaching.

Authored "Humans or Haptics: A Deep-dive into Robots for Sports Training", analyzing how robotic haptic feedback compares against human coaching in skill acquisition and perceptual memory.

The research was published in the National High School Journal of Science. You can read the full publication here on NHSJS.com.

Skills: Experimental Research, Biomechanical Analysis, Statistical Data Analysis, Scientific Publishing.
